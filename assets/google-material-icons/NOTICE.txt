Google Material Icons
Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
Licensed under Apache License 2.0; full license in LICENSE.
Modifications: original SVGs rasterized to 24x24 and thresholded at 50% coverage into 1-bit bitmaps.
Per-icon source URLs are recorded in manifest.json.

The converted bitmaps are embedded as static artwork in main/icons.h.
Bitmaps: 72 bytes/icon, 3 bytes/row, MSB first; 1 = foreground.
Host conversion uses existing Sharp and Pillow; no dependencies were installed.
